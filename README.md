# ⚡ Household Energy Optimizer

A **Smart Energy Consumption Dashboard** for households, schools, hospitals, mining, and industries.  
Predicts energy usage, estimates cost, and provides **smart advice** for efficient energy management.

---

## Features

- **Energy Prediction**: Enter appliance power & duration to predict total kWh.  
- **Cost Estimation**: Calculates approximate cost (TZS).  
- **Smart Advice**: Dynamic recommendations based on usage patterns.  
- **Visualization**: Bar chart showing energy distribution.  
- **Cloud-ready Background**: Appears only on Prediction page (embedded Base64).
